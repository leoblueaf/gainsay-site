Quorum.

One AI can sound certain
and still be wrong.

Quorum puts several frontier models in one room, holds them to rules that stop them agreeing with each other, and tells you at the end where they actually split.

Not four answers. One conversation.

Asking four chatbots the same question gets you four essays you have to reconcile yourself. Quorum runs one shared transcript: every model reads what the others said and has to respond to it. That is what turns four opinions into an argument, and an argument is the thing that has information in it.

Four house rules run on every reply, and they are the difference between a debate and a group nod:

  • Label the layer. Observation, inference or speculation, on every claim.
  • No artifact, no claim. A model with no source for a number has to say so.
  • Flag the performance urge. If it reaches for the impressive phrasing over the accurate one, it says so.
  • Add or disagree. Restating what someone else already said is not allowed.

The report is the point

When they are done, Quorum reads the whole transcript back and gives you the part worth acting on: where they split and what each one held, where they agreed without being asked, a verdict written for someone who was not in the room, and how confident it is with the reason in the same sentence.

If a disagreement matters, you can send it back to the models in it and make them defend or revise. Each one says what would prove it wrong.

You bring your own keys

Quorum requires your own API key from Google, OpenAI, Anthropic or xAI. The app talks to those companies directly from your phone using your account, so usage is billed to you at their prices. There is no subscription to us and no markup.

One key is enough to start, and Google's free tier does not ask for a card. Any seat without its own key is filled by a model you do have, always labelled, never pretending to be someone else.

You always know what it costs

A live meter runs while the models work. Documents and photos are priced before you send them, including the difference between the first turn and every turn after it. Typical conversations cost a few cents. Nothing is hidden and nothing is estimated after the fact.

No server. No account. No copy of your work.

Quorum has no backend. Your keys live in your phone's keychain, your conversations and documents live in one file on your device, and deleting the app deletes all of it. There is nothing on our side to breach, subpoena or sell, because there is no our side. See the privacy policy for the specifics.
